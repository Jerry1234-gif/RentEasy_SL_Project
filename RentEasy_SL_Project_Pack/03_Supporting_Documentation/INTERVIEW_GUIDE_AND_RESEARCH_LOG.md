# Stakeholder Interview Guide and Research Log

Use this guide for genuine interviews only. Obtain informed consent, explain the academic purpose, avoid collecting unnecessary personal data, and do not invent responses.

## Suggested participant groups
- Prospective tenants / people who have recently searched for accommodation.
- Landlords / property owners.
- Property agents or managers.

## Questions for tenants
1. How do you currently find rental properties?
2. What information do you need before contacting a landlord?
3. What difficulties have you experienced when comparing listings?
4. How do you arrange property viewings?
5. How do you determine whether an advertisement is trustworthy?
6. What information would you want to save or track?
7. What device and internet access do you normally use?
8. Which features would be most useful in a rental platform?

## Questions for landlords/agents
1. How do you advertise and manage available properties?
2. How do you record enquiries and viewing appointments?
3. How do you receive and assess rental applications?
4. How do you update listing availability?
5. How do you record maintenance issues?
6. What information should only be visible to authorised users?
7. What would make a digital platform useful or difficult to use?

## Analysis method
Group responses into themes (property discovery, listing accuracy, communication, viewings, applications, maintenance, trust and privacy). Compare themes across stakeholder groups. Convert supported needs into numbered requirements. Record disagreements and limitations. Do not claim the small interview sample represents all renters in Sierra Leone.
